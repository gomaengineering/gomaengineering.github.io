Kevin Earle - Earle Consulting

The UK government's commitment to net zero by 2050 underscores the urgency of addressing carbon emissions, a priority shared by over 300 local authorities declaring climate emergencies. Local authorities are increasingly embedding sustainability into procurement, rewarding suppliers who can demonstrate measurable carbon reduction and social value delivery.

Implementing a Sustainability Management System (SMS) as provided by Goma Engineering Consultation allows businesses to monitor Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions while addressing environmental, social, and economic responsibilities. By adopting this SMS, businesses not only reduce their carbon footprint but also align with their clients' sustainability goals, adding tangible value to both parties. This approach ensures a meaningful contribution to the shared journey toward net zero.
